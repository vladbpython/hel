## [2.2.1]

### Fixed

-**Batch sending no longer gets stuck on a full channel.**
  Previously, in a rare case, when the channel is completely clogged and people are already standing on it
  there are several senders in the queue, the batch entry `try_send_batch` could
  loop idle and waste the processor, instead of just
  indicate "no vacancies".
  Now it immediately returns `0` (“nothing sent”) and does not waste CPU.
  The problem was only with the MPMC channel.

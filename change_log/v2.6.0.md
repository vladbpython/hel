## [2.6.0]

### Added

- `Config::stall_takeover(Duration)`. Optional protection from a handler that blocks forever.
- Each worker has a heartbeat of finished items and a busy flag.
- A worker is stalled when it is busy and finishes nothing within the budget.
- The monitor moves the shards of a stalled worker to a live one and spawns a spare worker when needed.
- Nothing is lost and order is kept. The shard whose batch sits in the stuck worker stays with it, all other shards carry no data in flight.
- The frozen shard simply rejects new sends until the handler returns. Backpressure instead of silent loss.
- An idle worker is never marked as stalled. A slow worker is unmarked as soon as its item finishes.
- A stalled worker does not count as capacity and cannot get shards back from rebalance.
- Off by default. Without it strict ownership works exactly as before.
- Needs at least two possible workers. With `max_consumers` of one the constructor returns `PoolError::Config` instead of accepting a setting that can never act.

### Fixed

- `Duration::MAX` in `send_timeout` and `recv_timeout` no longer blocks forever. The value is clamped to the farthest representable deadline, the call stays alive and reacts to items, free space and disconnect. Documented on every timeout method.
- Panic inside an item `Drop` no longer aborts ring cleanup. Each slot is dropped under its own guard, the rest of the items are freed. Checked under Miri, no leaks and no double drops.
- `shard_power_of_two` with a huge shard count no longer wraps to zero shards. Both power of two helpers are safe for any input.
- SPSC `queued()` no longer returns garbage like `usize::MAX` and `is_empty()` no longer lies. The producer publishes `tail` before the slot sequence, the reader can never observe head ahead of tail. Pinned by a loom model, throughput unchanged.

### Compatibility

- `sync_pool_slot` and `async_pool_slot` now return `Result<Pool, PoolError>`. Add `?` or `.unwrap()` at the call site.
- Empty receiver list is now `PoolError::ReceiverEmpty` instead of a panic.
- Everything else is unchanged, channels and macros keep the same API.

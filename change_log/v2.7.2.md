## [2.7.2]

### Changed

- Producers that collide on one shard now step back instead of retrying instantly. When two senders reach for the same slot, the loser waits a few nanoseconds, doubling the pause on every retry. After a short spin budget it yields the core, because at that point the winner is most likely not running and spinning cannot help it. One sender alone pays nothing, the pause fires only on a real collision.
- `try_send` semantics did not change. A full channel still returns the error immediately with the value inside. The pause covers only the tiny window while another sender finishes writing its slot.
